3

Wren moment

When you're Wren, it feels like winning. Somebody always pays.

New money buys at old prices for whoever spends it first. By the time it reaches everyone else, prices have gone up. Whoever saved the old money pays the difference, just like the pentaja savers of Ulm Hollow.

In real life: when lots of new euros or dollars are made, the people who get them first spend them at today's prices. Your savings meet tomorrow's prices.
