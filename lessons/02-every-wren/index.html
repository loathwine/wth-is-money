Wren moment

By later accounts, the islanders spotted their Wren: O'Keefe.

On Yap, everyone knew each stone's story. So O'Keefe's new stones could be priced lower, and old savings stayed safe.

In Ulm Hollow, a pentaja is a pentaja. Nobody could tell Wren's from yours, so Wren's cave made everyone's pentaja worth less.

In real life: a euro is a euro too. When lots of new ones are made, nobody can tell them apart, and every euro in your savings buys a bit less.

Yap's money had a memory. Most money doesn't. Next: glass beads, where this time you are Wren.
