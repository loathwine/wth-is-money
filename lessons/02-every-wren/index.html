Rod moment

The islanders spotted their Rod.

O'Keefe's stones were easy to get, so, by later accounts, they traded for less than the old ones that had cost lives. Same as Ulm Hollow moving from pentaja to zirnet: when money gets easy to make, people quietly stop treating it as money.
